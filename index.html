What I’m growing toward

The common thread has always been people: knowing them well, connecting them, and keeping a lot of moving pieces organized.

I learned sales in person, walking into bars, restaurants, and liquor stores, building relationships face to face, following up until the answer was yes, and learning how to read and work a room. I sold by developing real relationships and hosting creative events and activations.

I’m excited by rising CPG brands. I love discovering new products on the shelf, talking to founders handing out their own samples at pop-ups, and seeing the hustle it takes to build a brand. I want to show up for those founders and help them build the relationships that grow their business.

Before Haas, I led the New York market for 818 Tequila, managing hundreds of relationships. I came to Haas to immerse myself in the startup ecosystem and be around motivated people creating new things. I hope to bring my skill set to startups by connecting founders with the right people, building the networks and programming that help companies grow, and making an ecosystem feel like a community.

Most recently, I pitched an events series to the Haas VC Club to elevate Berkeley’s investor and founder ecosystem, and was approved to host my first happy hour in early December. I was also selected for the Hospitality & VIP Experience role at the Women in Leadership Conference in February 2027. I’m motivated to build my reputation here as a community builder.
